## How *LoopDocs* is Organized

The tabs across the top of each page (or the menu on a phone) are in the order you use them: check the requirements in Intro, build the app, set it up, operate it, and troubleshoot. The Version, *Nightscout* and FAQs tabs hold reference information.

## How to Use *LoopDocs*

### Using Links

* A link with an arrow pointing up and to the right opens in a new tab or window. For example, the [What is Loop?](https://youtu.be/64qhgnmkyAE) video opens on *YouTube*.
* Other links open in the same tab. Use your browser's back button to return.
* To open any link in a new tab, right-click it (Control-click on a *Mac* without a right button). On a phone, press and hold the link.

### Using Tooltips

Words with a dashed underline have a definition. Hover over the word, or tap it on a phone, to see it. For example, Omnipod has a tooltip.

Every definition is also in the [Glossary](../faqs/glossary.md).

### Website Short Cuts

Type `loopdocs.org` or `looptips.org` in your browser to go to *LoopDocs* or *LoopTips*.

### Website Navigation

!!! tip "Keyboard Navigation"
    On a computer, you can use these keys:
    
    * `n` for next page
    * `p` for previous page
    * `s` for search

* On a computer (window wider than 1220 pixels):
    * The tabs across the top are the sections of *LoopDocs*
    * The pages in the current tab are listed on the left
    * The headings on the current page are listed on the right
* On a phone or narrow window:
    * Tap the menu icon (three lines) to see the tabs
    * Tap a tab to see its pages; tap the back arrow to return to the tabs
    * To see the headings on the current page, open the menu and tap the highlighted page

### Website Search

Most questions are already answered in *LoopDocs*. Tap the search icon (upper right), or type `s` on a computer, then type a word or phrase. As you type, matching pages are listed. Pick the one that answers your question.
